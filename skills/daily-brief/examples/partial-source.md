# Example: partial coverage (a revoked/unavailable account)

A broken account must never block a healthy one. Here the **Work** account needs reconnection, so
its Gmail/Calendar are `unavailable`, while **Personal** is `complete`. The brief is produced from
the healthy account and the gap is stated plainly. Payload:
`../../../evals/expected/07-revoked-work-account.json`.
