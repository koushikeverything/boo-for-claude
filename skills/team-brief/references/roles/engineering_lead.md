# Role pack — engineering_lead

**Top-of-mind ranking:** `incidents > code > tracking > chat > observability` (today first). An active
incident you own ranks first even with no effort estimate.

| Capability | Top of mind | FYI subgroup | Calendar |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|----------|
| incidents (PagerDuty) | active incidents on your team/services; on-call handoff | **Alerts** | on-call handoff |
| code (GitHub/GitLab) | stale PRs blocking merges; review requests; failing CI on release branches | **Reviews**, **Deploys** | — |
| tracking (Jira/Linear) | sprint-at-risk; blocked stories; scope decisions | **Updates** | sprint planning |
| chat (Slack/Teams) | team blocked on your decision; escalations to you | **Updates** | — |
| observability (Datadog/Sentry) | error spikes/regressions on your services since deploy | **Alerts** | — |

**Actions (drafts/preview only; approval-gated; attended only for acks):** draft replies, draft a PR
review comment (pending, not submitted), preview calendar event, acknowledge an incident (attended).
Never merge/close/resolve autonomously.

**Coverage:** code (mandatory) or tracking (mandatory) or incidents (mandatory) missing → blocking gap.
