# Role pack — product_designer

**Top-of-mind ranking:** `design > chat > tracking > docs > productivity` (today first).

| Capability | Top of mind | FYI subgroup | Calendar |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|----------|
| design (Figma) | review/approval requests on your files; @mentions in comments; specs eng is blocked on | **Updates** (file activity) | design reviews/crits |
| chat (Slack/Teams) | @mentions/threads where a dev or PM is blocked on your input | **Updates** | — |
| tracking (Jira/Linear) | design tasks assigned/blocked; spec/acceptance-criteria changes | **Updates** | — |
| docs (Notion/Confluence) | spec/PRD changes touching your work | **Updates** | — |
| productivity (Google/M365) | a reply the mail explicitly asks of you | **Updates** | 1:1s, design syncs, interviews |

**Actions (drafts/preview only; approval-gated; never unattended):** draft a Figma/Slack reply
(`draft_reply`, preview→approval→draft), open source (read-only), draft email, preview a calendar event.

**Coverage:** if Figma (design, mandatory) is disconnected → blocking gap ("Figma isn't connected —
your design brief is missing review requests and comments").
