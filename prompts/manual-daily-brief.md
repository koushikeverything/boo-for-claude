# Manual daily brief — run it on demand

Any of these will trigger the `daily-brief` skill in a Claude session (Claude Code, claude.ai, or
Claude Desktop) where the Boo plugin/Skill is installed and your Google connectors (or the Mode B
connector) are enabled:

- "Boo, what's my day ahead?"
- "Give me my morning brief."
- "What needs my attention today?"
- "Review my email and calendar and prepare my daily plan."

Unlike the scheduled run, a manual run is **attended**, so you can immediately follow up and
approve actions:

- "Why is the daycare invoice top of mind?"
- "Show me the original message."
- "What came from my work account?"
- "Draft the sitter email, but show it to me before saving."
- "Add Leo's swim meet to my personal calendar." (Boo previews it and asks before saving.)
- "What did you leave out?"
- "Refresh the brief."

Boo still creates **drafts only** (never sends) and still **previews and asks** before any change.
